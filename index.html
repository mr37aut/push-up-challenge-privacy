Privacy Policy - Push Up Challenge

Effective date: September 10, 2026

1. Introduction

MAG Production respects your privacy. The app "Push Up Challenge" is designed to count push-ups and works completely offline. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data.

2. Data Collection

"Push Up Challenge" does not collect or process any personal data.

  • No names or email addresses
  • No location data
  • No contacts
  • No photos or videos
  • No audio recordings
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No health or fitness data
  • No usage or tracking data

3. Data Sharing

The app does not share any data with third parties. No personal data is transmitted to MAG Production, external companies, advertising providers, analytics services, or other third parties.

4. Data Storage

The app does not store personal data. The app operates entirely offline and does not use external servers or cloud services.

5. Internet Access

"Push Up Challenge" does not require an internet connection. The app does not transmit data over the internet.

6. Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the functionality of the app changes in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

8. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the app, you can contact MAG Production through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.

This Privacy Policy applies to the "Push Up Challenge" app developed by MAG Production.
